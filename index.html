IDLE VINYL

About

IDLE Vinyl is a simulation game where you progress through scratching vinyls, buying upgrades and fighting robots!

Mechanics

Scratch the vinyl to progress. Buy upgrades, reach new ranks and feel as real DJ.

Customization and Pets

Impress your friends with your own customizable look! Meet various pet friends that will help you in battles!

Plot

In dark times when robots are seeking to destroy everything human. You, as a young DJ apprentice, must rise up and fight to save mankind's music!

Other

Unlock various vinyls with own background music. Open lucky box and play some minigames to get extra rewards!
